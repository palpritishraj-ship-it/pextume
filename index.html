FAQ Guide

Q: Are CGST and SGST always equal?
Yes, for intra-state transactions, total GST is divided equally (50-50%) between Central and State governments.

Q: Is this tool accurate for business?
Yes, it uses standard financial rounding for exact tax splitting.
