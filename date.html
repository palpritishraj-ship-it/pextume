How to Use

Date Duration: Select Start & End dates to find exact duration.

Add/Subtract Date: Choose a start date, input years/months/days, select Add(+) or Subtract(-), and find your future or past target date.

Time Duration: Calculate exact duration between two precise dates and times (including seconds). Supports 12/24 Hr format & Live Clocks.

FAQ Guide

Q: Does it support leap years?
Yes! It automatically adjusts for leap years and correct days in each month for flawless accuracy.

Q: Can I use live time tracking?
Yes! You can enable the Live Clock toggle in the Time Duration tab to track exact real-time seconds.
