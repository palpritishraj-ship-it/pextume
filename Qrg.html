❓ FAQ Guide

Are these QR codes free for commercial use?

Yes! Every QR code generated through Pextume is 100% free for both personal and commercial projects without any limits.

Do these QR codes ever expire?

Never. Since they are static codes created locally in your browser, they remain active and functional permanently.

Why is my QR code not scanning?

Ensure there is strong contrast between the QR pattern and the background color. Also, avoid using a very large center logo that covers too much of the code's data blocks.

Can I use my own brand logo?

Yes! Use the center logo uploader to easily embed your brand icon right in the middle of the QR code.
